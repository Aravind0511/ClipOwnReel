Downloader Settings

Configure optional session authentication

Instagram limits media downloads when requests are sent without user authentication. Adding your Instagram sessionid cookie lets you extract Reels, Posts, and Stories seamlessly.

How to get your sessionid:

  1. Log in to Instagram.com in your browser.
  2. Press F12 (or right click > Inspect) and go to Application > Cookies.
  3. Find and copy the value of sessionid.
  4. Paste it here and click Save.
